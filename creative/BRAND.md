# Captionflow Brand DNA

This document describes the visual constants of Captionflow. It is a boundary, not a template.

## Brand feeling

Captionflow should feel:

- premium
- precise
- calm under speed
- technical without looking cold
- minimal without looking empty
- modern without chasing trends
- creator-focused without looking playful or childish

The visual language sits between high-end product design and professional editing software.

## Core palette

Primary darks:
- near-black background: `#050509` to `#08090C`
- product surface: `#0B0C12` to `#0E1015`
- elevated surface: `#11131B` to `#151821`

Text:
- primary: `#F7F7F8` / `#FAFAFF`
- secondary: white around 42–58% opacity
- tertiary/metadata: white around 24–34% opacity

Brand accents:
- primary violet: `#7357FF`
- product violet: `#795CFF`
- hover violet: `#8C74FF`
- Captionflow blue: `#69ADFF`
- landing blue: `#65A8FF`
- optional pink endpoint: `#E16CFF` / `#F26BFF`

## Signature gradients

Approved Captionflow Signature families:

- Flow: `#B2A6FF → #765CFF`
- Ocean: `#6EC5FF → #5869FF`
- Aurora: `#8268FF → #DF6FFF`
- Rose: `#DB6FFF → #FF789B`

Use them as highlights, active states, caption emphasis, light accents or transition energy. Do not wash entire scenes in gradients by default.

## Typography

Product/marketing character:

`-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "SF Pro Display", "SF Pro Text", "Helvetica Neue", "Segoe UI Variable Display", Arial, sans-serif`

Typography principles:
- very strong negative tracking on large display type
- compact line-height
- medium rather than ultra-heavy display weight
- small metadata can use monospaced type
- hierarchy comes from scale, spacing and opacity more than many font weights

Suggested visual behavior:
- display: 560–680 visual weight
- large titles: tracking around `-0.05em`
- labels: small uppercase with generous tracking
- body text: quiet, neutral and readable

## Borders and surfaces

Captionflow rarely uses hard bright outlines.

Typical border language:
- subtle: white 6–9% opacity
- stronger: white 12–17% opacity
- active accent border: violet around 30–45% opacity

Surfaces:
- dark translucent panels
- faint top-left or top-edge light
- mild backdrop blur when useful
- strong black depth shadow rather than bright glow

Rounded geometry should feel controlled and consistent, generally between compact 8–16px controls and larger 20–28px product containers.

## Glow

Glow exists to indicate focus, energy or depth.

Good:
- soft violet halo behind a hero UI
- tiny luminous active-state dot
- low-opacity blue/violet atmospheric light
- controlled shadow under highlighted caption text

Bad:
- every object glowing
- huge neon bloom
- cyberpunk edges
- rainbow lighting with no UI reason

## Background language

Use near-black as the dominant field.

Optional environmental details:
- extremely subtle grid or point field
- one soft radial violet source
- one weaker blue source
- thin gradient hairline
- large blurred atmospheric ellipse

These should remain background information, never become the subject.

## Brand signature

The strongest recurring Captionflow visual signature is the relationship between:

**near-black space + crisp white typography + restrained translucent UI + violet/blue active energy.**

That relationship should survive even when a video has an entirely new composition.
